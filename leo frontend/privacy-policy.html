Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

This page explains how Leo's Pet Barkery handles customer data across shopping, orders, support, and account features.

Overview

We keep your details safe and use them only to serve your order

We collect information you share when you create an account, place an order, contact support, or browse the store. This may include your name, phone number, email address, shipping address, and order history.

We use this information to process orders, support delivery, improve the website, and send order-related updates. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Account details, contact info, shipping addresses, order history, and basic browsing activity.

How we use it

To fulfill orders, manage your account, respond to questions, and improve the shopping experience.

Sharing

We share only what is needed with delivery, payment, or service partners to complete your request.

Your choices

You can update account details, request support, or ask us to remove data where applicable.
